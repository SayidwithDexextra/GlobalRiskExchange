How risk moves today

Risk runs through banks.Most of it never gets a market.

Deep markets exist for a handful of big things. For almost everything else, a hedge is a private deal on the bank's terms, or not possible at all. Dexetera replaces the bank's role with open software and public data.

0.25 to 1%+ Typical cost hidden in a bank's spread on a bespoke hedge. DEXETERA: ~0.10% FEE→
~2% Typical fee per trade on popular prediction markets. DEXETERA: ~0.10% FEE→
Platform only Who decides which new markets get listed on most venues. DEXETERA: ANYONE LISTS→
Institutions Who gets access to a bank's hedging desk. DEXETERA: ANYONE TRADES→
