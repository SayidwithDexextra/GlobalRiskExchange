Questions

Before youwrite to us.

Can you help me find a market for my exposure?+

Yes. Tell us what moves your costs or revenue. We'll help you check whether a credible, public, archivable data source tracks it, and whether it fits as a market on Dexetera.

Do I need a crypto wallet to talk to you?+

No. Start with a conversation. When you're ready to trade, positions use USDC held in your own wallet, and creating a market or placing orders can be gasless.

Is there a minimum size?+

There is no dealer setting a minimum ticket. Positions are sized in contracts on a public on-chain orderbook, so you can start small.

How is a market settled?+

Each market settles against its named public data source. Anyone can propose the final value with evidence, anyone can challenge it with a bond, and disputes go to UMA's Optimistic Oracle.
